## Addendum, 14 October 2026: why the service pages are not indexed

Checked from the shell and in Search Console on 14 October 2026.

- All six service pages return 200, carry `index, follow`, a self-referencing canonical, a clean H1 ("Web design in Cyprus"), a title, a description, four JSON-LD blocks, and 1,050 to 1,350 words of copy in the raw HTML. They are in the sitemap (25 URLs) and linked twice each from the homepage HTML. robots.txt allows everything but /api/. Nothing on the site is blocking them.
- Search Console's Page indexing report (data dated 9 October) lists exactly seven URLs under "Discovered, currently not indexed": the six service pages and /privacy. Last crawled: N/A for all seven. Google has never fetched them.
- URL inspection on 14 October says "URL is unknown to Google" for each service page. A live test of /services/web-design returned "URL is available to Google", page can be indexed, breadcrumbs valid. Googlebot can fetch and render the page without issue.
- Indexing was requested for all six service pages on 14 October (priority crawl queue). The /services hub is indexed already.

Reading: this is not a technical fault and not a content fault. "Discovered, not indexed, never crawled" on a site whose real link profile is single digits means Google has not spent crawl budget on the new URLs. The site was replaced in late August with new URLs, the only external links are flagged spam, and nothing outside the site points at the service pages. The manual request moves them into a queue; it does not guarantee indexing. If they are still unknown in two weeks, the next lever is external links to the service pages themselves (Clutch profile links, the Google Business Profile website link, directory listings, client site footers) and nothing on-page.
