# Article 6: professional web agency pricing

Checked: 10 October 2026. Author: Konstantinos Kyprianou. [Draft](../blog-drafts/web-design-agency-pricing.md).

## Pricing provenance

All studio amounts come from the explicit owner-confirmed pricing record dated 3 October 2026 in [the earlier blog plan](../blog-plan-2026.md), line 80 at drafting. That later confirmed record supersedes older keyword-research estimates and the obsolete €1,000/€2,000/€4,000 bands. These are studio prices, not Cyprus or global market averages.

- One-page: from €1,200.
- Design only: €1,800; development only: €1,800. The article presents these as starting budgets, not additive components of the combined price.
- Combined design and development: €2,500 minimum for a standard site with five main pages.
- Redesign: from €2,500; 3D: from €6,000.
- Hosting and maintenance: €40 per month; €90 with a CMS.

The record does not authorize numerical add-on prices. No CMS setup increment, extra-page price, translation surcharge, day rate or hours estimate was invented. VAT treatment, subscription inclusion, usage and support limits are left to the project quote because their precise commercial terms are not established by the record. The draft can be reviewed as complete without deciding those terms here. Reconfirm rates during ordinary publication review if publication is materially later.
