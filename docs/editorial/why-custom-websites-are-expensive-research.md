# Article 5: why custom websites cost more

Checked: 10 October 2026. Author: Konstantinos Kyprianou. [Draft](../blog-drafts/why-custom-websites-are-expensive.md).

## Distinct search purpose

Explains the labor and requirements behind a custom website, and how a business can assess whether that work creates value. Article 6 owns actual studio pricing, proposal comparison and procurement. This article is not a second price list or the later redesign-cost article.

The supplied hook says cheap templates break, run slowly and cost more over time. Those are not universal facts. The draft retains the investment argument while explaining the conditions under which a template or custom build makes sense. Custom code, high prices and visually elaborate work are not treated as quality guarantees.

The consultancy example, proposal example and ownership-cost framework are clearly editorial or hypothetical. The €1,000 and €2,500 comparison figures illustrate a €1,500 initial difference; they do not represent a client quote or prove eventual savings. No invented add-on increments, time estimates or lost-revenue values appear.

The full-cost comparison includes operating expenses and team time, but does not assign speculative monetary value to missed enquiries. Brand and engineering recommendations are author judgments rather than findings attributed to the linked technical sources.
