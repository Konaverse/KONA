# Article 7: can ChatGPT create a website?

Checked: 10 October 2026. Author: Konstantinos Kyprianou. [Draft](../blog-drafts/can-chatgpt-create-a-website.md).

## Capability research

Used the OpenAI Docs skill for current product capabilities, searched official documentation and opened the matching [ChatGPT Sites documentation](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/sites). The page describes creating, hosting, refining and sharing websites and web apps. It is a public-beta workflow with plan-dependent availability and limits at the research date. The article uses the verified broad capability without copying volatile plan lists or promising account access.

The planner's claim that AI can only generate basic code and cannot create unique aesthetics, optimize assets or implement complex interactions is rejected. This article assesses delivery evidence and business requirements. It does not claim AI is technically incapable of those tasks, or that AI-generated sites are intrinsically insecure.

No website was built or tested for this article, and no AI tool was benchmarked against an agency. The enquiry scenario and launch checks are proposed evaluation methods, not reported experimental results. No claim is made that Konaverse currently uses a particular AI tool in client delivery.

The legal answer is general and distinguishes permission to use a tool from asset rights, ownership and data handling. It does not declare a particular site's legal status. The U.S. Copyright Office Part 2 report was also consulted during research; no U.S. conclusion is presented as Cyprus law, and it is not needed to support the final draft.
