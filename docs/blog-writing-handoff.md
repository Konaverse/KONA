## Voice and style to preserve

The detailed [voice and search standard](editorial/konstantinos-voice-and-search.md) records the five complete 2020 Medium samples, access limits and primary search references. The writing samples were read through the public RSS feed; two additional February articles were available only as extracts. Do not claim that every article on the profile was read.

Write as a technical founder helping one business owner make a decision. Speak directly to “you” and “your team.” Use polished grammar while retaining the warmth, ambition and practical reasoning of the original samples. Authority comes from a clear recommendation, its reasons, useful evidence and an honest account of its limits.

- Use “I” for the author's judgment and standards. Use “we” for verified studio facts. Neither pronoun licenses an invented memory, client conversation or result.
- Explain mechanisms through everyday consequences: an article waiting for a developer, a booking tap that feels unresponsive, a proposal whose scope is unclear.
- Give examples enough detail to clarify the decision. Mark hypothetical businesses, scenarios and calculations clearly. Treat real brand statements as their own accounts unless independent evidence supports more.
- Build connected paragraphs with varied sentence length. Use short sentences selectively. Vary openings between articles and avoid repeating the same analogy, rhetorical pattern or first-person phrase.
- Keep technical language proportionate to the reader's decision and explain unfamiliar terms. Let expertise show through precision.
- Use tables for useful comparisons, lists for actual steps and questions for decisions. Avoid making every section a list or every paragraph a slogan.
- End with a concrete action: a handover test, proposal check, measurement plan or brief. Give one relevant Konaverse service link a natural reason to appear.
- Keep the established sign-off, “Until next time.” It belongs to the author voice; it is not an SEO tactic.

Avoid inflated promises, forced keyword repetition, generic trend introductions, adversarial claims about competitors and unsupported statements that custom code, AI, motion or luxury styling always produces a particular outcome. Human character comes from specific reasoning and credible detail, not deliberate grammar errors or claims about defeating AI detectors.

## Research and SEO/GEO standards

General search guidance was researched on 10 October 2026. Article-specific research records are dated 10–11 October. This handoff consolidates that work; it is not a new verification of external sources. Recheck volatile information for each new article and when publication is delayed.

Use original papers, official product documentation, standards bodies, first-party reports and directly observed site behavior as appropriate to the claim. Record the exact supported finding and its boundary in the article's research note. Link sources next to the claims they support. A vendor document can establish a capability or its stated positioning; it does not independently establish buyer behavior, superiority or commercial results.

Record what was accessible: full text, relevant PDF sections, an abstract or indexed extracts. A blocked publisher page does not justify claiming to have read the full paper. A copy of the original paper can be used with its provenance recorded. Prefer current evidence when the subject is current; retain useful historical research with its actual date and context.

Keep these distinctions explicit:

- Research check date, source publication date and date of the underlying observations are separate.
- Controlled experiments, observational associations, conceptual definitions and editorial recommendations carry different kinds of support.
- Relative changes and percentage-point changes are different. State the denominator and distinguish sessions, people, events, enquiries, qualified leads and sales.
- An original checklist, comparison lens or fill-in brief is an authored recommendation unless it has actually been validated.
- Laboratory profiles, repository behavior and physical-device experience are separate evidence. Existing headless Chrome performance reports do not prove smoothness on a real device or Safari.

The recorded Google/Bing research supports useful, original content, clear authorship, readable text, internal discovery and accurate evidence. Search eligibility also depends on the published page's technical setup. There is no measured Konaverse ranking, citation, keyword-volume or conversion lift from this drafting work.

Answer the main question early and develop the consequence for the business. Give each article a distinct purpose and a practical contribution. Use clear headings and comparisons when they help the reader. These are editorial choices; there is no mandatory GEO layout, word count, keyword density, special AI markup or guarantee of citations. Do not add generic FAQPage schema as a GEO shortcut. Refresh reporting guidance and check the actual Search Console/Bing property before defining a measurement baseline.
