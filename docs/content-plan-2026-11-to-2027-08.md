# Konaverse content plan, November 2026 to August 2027

Written: 13 October 2026. Supersedes the unwritten half of the [three-month planner](content-planner-three-months.md) (Articles 13 to 24) and the topic order in the [2026 blog plan](blog-plan-2026.md). The [draft audit](editorial/drafts-audit-2026-10-13.md) records what happened to Articles 1 to 12.

Brand sentence, agreed 13 October 2026: Konaverse is an immersive web studio in Cyprus. Local SEO keeps the pipeline alive and builds the review base. The niche terms, the showcase and the citations build the premium side.

## The rule that governs this plan

No article is scheduled without a named source of real substance. Real substance means one of:

1. A Konaverse build the reader can open: a client site, Lumière Éclat, or a Labs concept piece.
2. A Konaverse number: a price, a timeline from a real project, a measurement from the repository's performance reports, the 172-URL migration record, a device test we ran.
3. An inspection we actually performed: a named site opened, screenshotted, measured and credited.
4. A decision the founders made and can explain in their own words.

The hypothetical-business pattern that carried Articles 2 to 12 is retired. If a topic has demand but no substance, it waits until the substance exists. That is why the plan is shorter than two a week: about 22 articles and 3 Labs pieces over ten months, which is the most a three-person studio can make real.

## Evidence behind the topic choices

- Keyword Planner ranges and difficulty from [the September research](keyword-research.md). These are September figures; Ahrefs was not reachable on 13 October. Refresh any figure before relying on it in copy.
- The 1,311 autocomplete suggestions in `keyword-research/suggest.md`. The strongest clusters by feed count are 3D website (examples, design, templates, builder), immersive website (examples, design), scrollytelling (websites, examples), WebGL website examples, Three.js websites, interactive website examples, one-page website examples, "what makes a website look professional" and the Cyprus set (agency, prices, services).
- The People Also Ask bank in `keyword-research.md` section 3.
- Live search checks on 13 October 2026 for who ranks: redesign-checklist and choose-an-agency queries are owned by mid-size agency blogs with interchangeable advice; best-3D-websites is owned by two or three small curators plus a template seller, with no authoritative list; the Blender-to-Three.js query is tutorials and courses, with no studio making-of in the top ten; "immersive website design agency" returns directory profiles and small studios, no editorial page; the Cyprus agency query returns Clutch, DesignRush, OneLittleWeb and two agency-written lists.
- The September AI-engine autopsy: agencies were quoted only when a sentence carried stack, price and timeline.

What the data cannot say: whether any of these pages will rank or be cited. No Konaverse non-brand ranking exists yet. The plan is a bet on demand plus substance, measured monthly.

## LinkedIn, not the blog

Each Labs piece, each making-of and each inspection produces short founder posts. Two a week is realistic there because the substance already exists. The blog carries the long record; LinkedIn carries the clips. Do not write LinkedIn-only opinion pieces to fill a calendar.

## Measurement

Baseline before the first November piece: Search Console export, Bing Webmaster Tools, local pack position for "web design cyprus" from Nicosia, Clutch Cyprus list position, prompt battery run one. Review monthly. The January 2027 review decides whether the Cyprus floor or the immersive ceiling is moving and shifts the mix accordingly. Nothing in this plan is a promise of rankings.
