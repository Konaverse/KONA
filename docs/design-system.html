Konaverse · design system v1

Whiteout,
single ice

Everything below is thin on purpose. Six type sizes, eight colours, four easing curves, ten components. A 6 to 8 page site does not need more, and every token you add is one more thing that drifts out of sync between Figma and the codebase.

The names here match the CSS file exactly, and they will match the Figma variables exactly. When a value changes it changes in the CSS first, then gets pushed to Figma. One direction, never both.

01 · Colour

Eight values,
seven roles

Components never reference a raw colour. They reference a role, so the whole site can be retuned by editing three lines.

white#FFFFFF
surface

mist#F4F6F7
surface-raised

silver#DDE3E7
hairline

graphite#687076
text-muted · 5.04:1 white, 4.65:1 mist

ink#15171A
text · 16.5:1

ice#7FA8C9
accent · light and rim only

ice-soft#DCE8F1
accent-wash · large fills

ice-deep#2E5F8A
text-accent · 6.1:1

Two rules that keep this from going wrong. Ice at #7FA8C9 fails contrast on white at 2.4:1, so it can never carry text or a small icon. It is light, rim and object tint only. When the accent has to be readable, that is ice-deep. And the accent appears at most four or five times on any page. If it starts showing up on every hover state it stops being a signal.

Why graphite is #687076 and not #6E767C. The original cleared AA on white at 4.62:1, but only reached 4.26:1 on mist — and since surface-raised is mist, muted copy was failing on every alternating section. This is the smallest darkening that clears 4.5 on both surfaces, and the two are indistinguishable side by side.

02 · Type

Manrope,
weight does the work

One family across the site, six sizes, nothing else. The whole personality comes from the jump between 200 and 600, which is why the mid weights stay out of headlines. There is deliberately no label or eyebrow style: no small caps above a heading, no section numbers, no captions in a second face. A section break is space and a hairline, nothing more.

04 · Motion

Five durations,
four curves

This is the part most systems skip, and it is the single biggest reason a site feels incoherent. Click each curve to feel it. Note that none of them overshoot: back and bounce easings would break the register completely.

d-instant 120msColour and opacity on hover. The only thing allowed to be fast.
d-quick 240msCursor, small state changes.
d-base 420msComponent-level movement.
d-slow 900msSection reveals. Structural.
d-cinema 1400msPage transitions and hero sequences.

05 · The signature

Refraction,
not fade

The one rule the whole identity rests on. Type does not fade up, it resolves from a displaced blurred state, as though coming into focus through moving glass. Press the button to replay it.

Reveal · d-slow · e-glass

Built as one continuous story
Section breaksNo labels anywhere. A break is --section-y of space plus one hairline. With nothing announcing a section, the vertical rhythm has to be genuinely even and the hairline has to be exact.
lens-scale1.06. Subtle by design. Anything above 1.12 turns into a gimmick.
lens-fringeSingle cool tone, ice at 42% opacity. Route B means one fringe, not two.
reveal-blur14px, resolving to 0 over 900ms.
reveal-shift18px of displacement. Small enough to read as focus rather than travel.
Blender rigOne key light with a cool rim. No dispersion setup needed under Route B, which keeps the render pipeline simple.
